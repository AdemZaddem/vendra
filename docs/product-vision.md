# Product Vision

## Working product description

A Tunisian social-commerce platform that helps Instagram and Facebook clothing sellers create an online storefront, manage products and inventory, accept Cash on Delivery or online payments, and fulfill orders from one dashboard.

## Target customer

Small Tunisian clothing merchants who currently sell through Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and manual spreadsheets/messages.

They usually need:

- A simple storefront link for their social profiles
- Product sizes and colours
- Cash-on-delivery order handling
- Better order and stock organization
- A checkout that works for Tunisian customers

## Core problem

Social sellers lose orders, oversell products, waste time answering repetitive messages, and struggle to track COD, delivery, customers, and inventory across chats and spreadsheets.

## Core promise

> Turn social traffic into managed COD and online-payment orders.

## How it works

A merchant adds products and variants, publishes their storefront, and shares the link on Instagram or Facebook. Customers browse, checkout with COD or online payment, and the merchant manages the full order lifecycle from one dashboard.

## What makes it local

- TND prices
- Tunisian phone numbers
- Governorate and delegation delivery addresses
- Cash-on-delivery-first workflow
- Tunisian payment-provider integration
- Delivery and COD operational tracking

## What it is not

- Not a marketplace
- Not a payment wallet
- Not a delivery company
- Not a platform that holds customer money
- Not an enterprise e-commerce suite in v1

## V1 success definition

A merchant can publish at least one product, share a storefront link, receive a real or test COD/online-payment order, and fulfill it without manually tracking the order in messages or spreadsheets.

## One-sentence pitch

> A simple Tunisian commerce operating system for social sellers to turn Instagram and Facebook traffic into organized, fulfillable orders.
